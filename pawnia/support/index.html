Frequently asked questions

How do I add a pet to Pawnia?

Open Pawnia and tap the + button on the home screen. Fill in your pet's basic info — name, species, breed, date of birth, photo — and tap Save. You can add multiple pets and edit their profiles at any time.

How do I create a Quick Card (groomer, vet, travel, etc.)?

Open your pet's profile, go to Documents and tap New card. Choose the card type — groomer, pet sitter, boarding, emergency, travel checklist, vet visit, lost-pet card — then select which fields to include and arrange them in the order you need.

Once saved, you can export the card as a PDF or PNG and share it via Messages, AirDrop, email or any other app.

Where is my data stored? Does Pawnia work offline?

Pawnia is built offline-first. All your pets' profiles and documents are stored locally on your device. The app works fully without an internet connection and your data is not uploaded to our servers.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Because data is stored locally, we recommend exporting your most important documents as PDF files before switching devices and saving them to iCloud, Google Drive or another cloud you trust.

A built-in backup & restore feature is on our roadmap and will be added in an upcoming update. If you need help in the meantime, email us at hello.tynkstudio@gmail.com.

How do I delete my data or remove the app?

Pawnia doesn't require registration and doesn't store your data on any server. To remove all data, simply delete the app from your device — everything stored locally will be removed with it.

You can also clear individual pets or documents from within the app: open the item, tap the menu (⋯) and choose Delete.

I found a bug or have a feature request. What should I do?

We'd love to hear from you. Send a short description (and a screenshot if it's a bug) to hello.tynkstudio@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version — that helps us reproduce issues faster.
