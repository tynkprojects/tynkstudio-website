Privacy Policy

How Pawnia handles your data. Short version: everything stays on your device — we don't collect, store, or share anything on our servers.

Effective Date: May 15, 2026

1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes what data the Pawnia application (the "Application") collects and processes, the purposes for which it is used, and the rights users have regarding their data.

By using the Application, you agree to the terms of this Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Application.

2. Data Controller

The data controller is the developer of the Pawnia application (referred to as "We" or the "Controller").

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at:

Email
tynkprojects@gmail.com

3. Data We Collect

3.1. Data You Provide

While using the Application, you may enter the following information about your pet:

  • Name, species, breed, sex, date of birth
  • Weight records (history of weigh-ins)
  • Behavioral journal entries (user-written observations)
  • Tasks and events (reminders, scheduled activities)
  • Uploaded documents (photos, files)

For the emergency pet card (optional), you may provide:

  • Diet type, allergies, medical conditions, special needs
  • Owner contact details (optional)
  • Emergency contacts (optional)
  • Veterinary clinic or doctor information (optional)
  • Additional notes

All emergency card data is provided voluntarily. You decide what to include.

3.2. Data Collected Automatically

When using the Application, the following may be processed automatically:

  • Device push token — used for sending local and push notifications (task and event reminders). This token is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

3.3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or process:

  • Your name, phone number, or address (unless you manually enter it in the emergency card)
  • Geolocation data
  • Login credentials (no authentication required in the current version)
  • Payment data (the Application is free)
  • Advertising identifiers

4. Data Storage

All data you enter in the Application is stored locally on your device. We do not use cloud servers to store user data.

Data you export (PDF, PNG, reports) is shared only through your device's standard system mechanisms (e.g., "Share," email, messaging apps) and only at your initiative.

We do not receive or store copies of exported files.

5. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

The Application uses open-source third-party libraries solely for functionality and interface purposes. None of them collect or transmit your personal data to third parties:

  • camera, image_picker — access to camera and gallery (only upon user request)
  • video_player, just_audio — media playback
  • path_provider, shared_preferences — local storage access
  • pdf, printing, flutter_native_html_to_pdf, html_to_image — PDF/PNG generation and export
  • share_plus — system sharing dialog
  • flutter_email_sender — opening email client
  • url_launcher — opening external links
  • fl_chart — charts and graphs
  • flutter_bloc, bloc, meta — state management (no data collection)
  • flutter_localizations, intl — localization
  • liquid_glass_renderer, material_symbols_icons, another_flushbar — UI components

None of these libraries perform analytics, advertising, or data transmission to external servers.

6. Purpose of Data Processing

The data you enter is processed exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Displaying and storing pet information on your device
  • Creating the pet emergency card
  • Generating weight charts and reports
  • Creating PDF/PNG documents and sharing them at your request
  • Sending reminders for tasks and events via notifications

7. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share your data with third parties, advertisers, analytics services, or government authorities, except where required by applicable law.

Exported data (PDF, PNG, reports) is shared only at your initiative through your device's system tools. You choose the recipient.

8. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access information about what data is stored in the Application
  • Correct or update your data directly within the Application
  • Delete all data by removing the Application from your device
  • Request confirmation of data deletion by contacting us at tynkprojects@gmail.com

Since all data is stored locally, deleting the Application results in complete deletion of all associated data.

9. Children

The Application is not intended for children under 4 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us at tynkprojects@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Policy. In case of significant changes, we will notify you via the Application or update the effective date.

Continued use of the Application after changes are published constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.
