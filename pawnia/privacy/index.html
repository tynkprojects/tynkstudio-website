This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") describes what data the Pawnia application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") collects and processes, the purposes for which it is used, and the rights users have regarding their data.

3. Data We Collect

3.1. Data You Provide

While using the Application, you may enter the following information about your pet:

  • Name, species, breed, sex, date of birth
  • Weight records (history of weigh-ins)
  • Behavioral journal entries (user-written observations)
  • Tasks and events (reminders, scheduled activities)
  • Uploaded documents (photos, files)

For the emergency pet card (optional), you may provide:

  • Diet type, allergies, medical conditions, special needs
  • Owner contact details (optional)
  • Emergency contacts (optional)
  • Veterinary clinic or doctor information (optional)
  • Additional notes

All emergency card data is provided voluntarily. You decide what to include.

3.2. Data Collected Automatically

When using the Application, the following may be processed automatically:

  • Device push token — used for sending local and push notifications (task and event reminders).

This token is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

With your optional consent, Pawnia uses Meta App Events to measure and improve advertising. Meta may receive installations, app launches, automatically detected in-app purchases, advertising identifiers where permitted, an SDK-generated identifier, IP address and device/app information. Pet records, photos, documents, contacts and the email used for purchase restoration are not included in these events.

3.3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or process:

  • Your name, phone number, or address (unless you manually enter it in the emergency card)
  • Geolocation data
  • Login credentials (the app does not require mandatory registration; optional email binding is used solely for purchase restoration)

4. Data Storage

All data you enter in the Application is stored locally on your device. We do not use cloud servers to store user data.

Data you export (PDF, PNG, reports) is shared only through your device's standard system mechanisms (e.g., "Share," email, messaging apps) and only at your initiative.

We do not receive or store copies of exported files.

If you voluntarily bind an email for Pawnia Pro purchase restoration, only your email and purchase information (plan, expiration date) are transmitted to the recovery server. All other pet data remains exclusively on your device. Email binding is optional — you can use the app and Pawnia Pro without it.
